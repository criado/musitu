## toki a!

+ You can print it and play [using this](./print_and_play.pdf)

+ Want a pretty version? You can [buy it here](https://www.thegamecrafter.com/games/musi-tu). That only contains 67 out of the 133 possible cards (otherwise it's too expensive). The other 66 cards  [can be bought here](https://www.thegamecrafter.com/games/musi-tu-b) with a different background so one can mix them and separate them later. The game has no markup price so it so it can be the cheapest. From the community to the community.

+ All sitelen pona are facing outwards. For symbols that look similar, we used different colors, so you don't have to rotate the images so much in your head.

+ **Recommended rules**: shuffle the cards and give one card to every player face down. On the count of three, everyone flips their card at the same time that the rest of the deck is placed face up. Players place their hand on the deck pile when they see the symbol in common between their card and the one at the top of the deck. They say the Toki Pona word out loud (or point at the symbol if they don't know it), and they take that card and place it on top of their previous card, becoming their next card. The game continues until the end and the one that won the most cards wins.
